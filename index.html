Use your own handpan recordings. Name them like D3.wav, F#4.mp3, or just ding.wav — the app matches filenames to pads and pitch-shifts neighbours. Files picked here stay on this device.
Watch the two bars under the waveform while you play a note: vol must fill past its marker, and onset must tick ✓ — on the strike attack or a clear jump in level. Raise sensitivity until both tick ✓ on soft strikes. From sensitivity 4 up, one clear note becomes a pad instantly; 1–3 asks for a second strike to confirm.
